# Sunrise (1927) - Emotional Scene Guide

Film: Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans
Director: F.W. Murnau
Archive: https://archive.org/details/Sunrise1927
Runtime: ~95 minutes
Copyright: Public domain (US, pre-1929)

## How to use this guide

1. Download the film from Internet Archive
2. Place it in `shorts/input/sunrise_1927.mp4`
3. Copy one of the spec templates below into `shorts/specs/`
4. Adjust timestamps to match your download (versions may differ by a few seconds)
5. Run: `python spark_shorts.py make specs/sunrise_guilt.json`

## Key emotional scenes (approximate timestamps)

### Theme: GUILT / BETRAYAL
- **0:08:00 - 0:09:30** — The Man meets the City Woman at night by the marshes. Secret affair, sneaking away from wife.
- **0:10:00 - 0:11:00** — City Woman seduces him, whispers "Come to the city." Moonlit embrace.
- **0:12:30 - 0:14:00** — City Woman suggests he drown his wife. His face shows horror then temptation.
- **0:15:00 - 0:16:30** — The Man returns home. Wife is innocent and happy. He can barely look at her. Guilt on his face.

### Theme: LOVE / INNOCENCE
- **0:06:00 - 0:07:00** — Wife tending to their baby. Domestic peace. She doesn't know.
- **0:20:00 - 0:22:00** — Boat ride across the lake. Wife trusts him completely. He almost does it but stops.
- **0:35:00 - 0:40:00** — Reconciliation in the church. They watch a wedding, remember their own vows. Both cry.
- **0:42:00 - 0:45:00** — Walking through the city together, reborn. Traffic stops for them (famous scene).

### Theme: HEARTBREAK / LOSS
- **0:22:00 - 0:24:00** — After he almost drowns her. She runs. His face when he realizes what he nearly did.
- **1:15:00 - 1:20:00** — Storm on the lake. Wife is swept away. He searches desperately.
- **1:20:00 - 1:25:00** — Village searches through the night. He sits alone, devastated.

### Theme: REDEMPTION
- **1:25:00 - 1:28:00** — Wife is found alive. Reunion. Pure relief and joy.
- **0:35:00 - 0:38:00** — Church scene where they silently recommit to each other.

## Notes
- Timestamps are approximate and may shift 30-60s depending on the archive version
- The film is silent, so "original audio" is just the musical score
- You'll want to write strong story subtitles since there's no dialogue to hear
- Hook ideas: "He planned to kill her for another woman..." / "She didn't know about the boat ride..." / "He almost did it..."
