### ❌ What's wrong
1. **No niche.** "Daily vlog plus cinematic POV of the world" is one of the most crowded formats on YouTube.
2. **No answer to "why would someone watch?"** The idea itself says *"I don't know what to create…"*. That's the real blocker, not cameras or editing.
3. **Daily vlogs are a treadmill.** They need daily output with no compounding value, and most channels quit by video 20.

### 🤔 Why it's wrong
- The idea starts from a **format** (POV) instead of a **subject** or **audience**. Channels grow on a clear promise ("I'll show you X"), not on a camera angle.
- Without a story that runs across videos, every video starts from zero views.

### 🔧 How to make it right
1. **Make the channel about building your other ideas.** POV is perfect for that:
   - *"Following my own biryani trail across Chennai"*: content for Nest, and every video links to a public trail.
   - *"Building a robot from zero electronics knowledge"* (RoboLab).
   - *"I put 10 AI agents in a Bigg Boss house"* (NPC Lab).
2. **Pick one series and commit to 12 episodes.** Weekly, not daily.
3. **Shorts first:** 30–60 second POV clips find an audience cheaply, and long-form comes later.
4. **Measure** average view duration and subscribers per video, not total views.

### 🌍 The space
| Player type | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Indian food-walk channels (city food crawls) | Food POV works in India, but the winners have a persona and a series |
| "Build in public" creators | An audience follows a *journey* with stakes. Your ideas give you that |
| Generic daily vloggers | Huge supply, low differentiation. Avoid |
