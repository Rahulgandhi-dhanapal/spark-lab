# Get-more-reviews QR card and WhatsApp thank-you nudge

**The spark** (one or two lines)
A table QR card that opens the shop's "write a review" page, plus a Tamil thank-you WhatsApp message that asks happy customers for a review the same day. Bundle with review replies at Rs 799 a month.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: happy customers leave without reviewing; unhappy ones always review.
2. Buyer: the same shops as review replies.
3. Edge: Tamil message, same-day ask, part of one bundle.
4. Fit: a small web page per shop plus WhatsApp templates.

**First step, if I were to try it**
5 shops for 4 weeks. Yes = their new reviews per week double.
