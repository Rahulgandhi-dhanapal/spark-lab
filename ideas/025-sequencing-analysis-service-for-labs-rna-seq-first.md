# Sequencing analysis service for labs (RNA-seq first)

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Sequencing analysis service for college and research labs, starting with bulk RNA-seq (which genes go up or down), with a clear AI-assisted report. Later a web tool: upload data, get the report.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: labs get sequencing data and nobody can analyse it; data sits for months.
2. Buyer: PhD scholars, professors, small biotech startups; paid per project (about Rs 5,000-10,000 to start).
3. Edge: biotech major who can code, plus plain-language reports and paper-ready figures.
4. Fit: Salmon + DESeq2 run on a laptop; public data (NCBI GEO) for learning.
5. Avoid human patient data at first (DPDP, ethics).

**First step, if I were to try it**
8-week plan: reproduce 2 published datasets, build a sample report, talk to 15 people, deliver 3 projects. Pass = 2 of 3 come back or refer. Plan doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/X7zM4k9h5zWozgVyXjT1qM
