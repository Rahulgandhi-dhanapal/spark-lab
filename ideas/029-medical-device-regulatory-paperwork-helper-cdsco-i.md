# Medical device regulatory paperwork helper (CDSCO, ISO 13485)

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Regulatory paperwork helper for small medical device startups: files for India's medical device rules (CDSCO), ISO 13485 quality system and ISO 14971 risk management.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: consultants are expensive and startups can't sell without these documents.
2. Buyer: small device startups; high value per customer.
3. Edge: AI drafting plus a regulatory expert partner.
4. Watch-outs: mistakes have legal consequences; never without an expert who signs off.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Partner with a regulatory consultant, help on 2 client files, measure hours saved.
