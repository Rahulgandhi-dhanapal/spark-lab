# Domain advisor in Tamil (farming, law, health or accounting)

**The spark** (one or two lines)
A small model or retrieval system built on one field's documents that answers in that field's vocabulary, for example a farming advisor that takes a crop problem and suggests likely causes and fixes.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Specialised assistants beat general chatbots on narrow tasks and can run cheaply.
2. Local-language advice is underserved.
3. The edge is exclusive, good domain data and trusted sources.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Pick one field where we already have contacts and gather 50 real questions with trusted answers.
