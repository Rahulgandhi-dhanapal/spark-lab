# LocalAI: AI setup service for small Chennai businesses

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Set up WhatsApp booking, Tamil and English auto-replies, and invoice and inventory automation for clinics, salons, tuition centres and restaurants.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Every small business will need AI, and very few know how to set it up.
2. Big companies sell software; nobody sits with the clinic receptionist and makes it work.
3. Cash from month one, and we learn how real businesses run (useful for Nest: restaurants are users too).

**First step, if I were to try it**
Do it free for one clinic or salon we know, then charge the second one.

### ❌ What's wrong
1. **"AI for small businesses" is too broad.** A clinic, a salon and a tuition centre need different things, so every client becomes a custom project.
2. **The tools are getting easier fast.** Meta keeps adding AI and automation to WhatsApp Business, and a dozen Indian platforms sell WhatsApp bots. The "setup" gap shrinks every year.
3. **Small businesses pay little and leave easily.** ₹1–3k a month, cancelled the first slow month.
4. **Support load:** when the bot misbehaves at 9 pm, the clinic calls you.

### 🤔 Why it's wrong
- "AI" is the tool, not the value. A clinic owner doesn't buy AI; they buy **fewer no-shows** and **no missed calls**.
- Without one vertical, you never build reusable software, so revenue stays tied to your hours.

### 🔧 How to make it right
1. **Pick one vertical.** Small **clinics** (dental, physio, paediatric) are the best fit: appointments, reminders, follow-ups, Tamil voice notes from patients. The pain is clear and measurable (no-shows cost real money), and clinic owners pay more than shops.
2. **Sell an outcome:** "We cut no-shows by 30% or you don't pay for month 2."
3. **Standardise after client 3:** the same flow, templates and dashboard for every clinic, which is your product starting to form.
4. **Charge a setup fee plus a monthly fee,** never free after the first pilot.
5. **Build on the official WhatsApp Business Platform** through a provider, not unofficial tricks, or numbers get banned.

### 🌍 The space
| Player | What they do | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp bot platforms (e.g. Interakt, WATI, AiSensy) | Self-serve tools for businesses | Build on them. Your value is setup and the vertical workflow |
| Practo and clinic software | Discovery and clinic management for doctors | Big, but weak on small-clinic WhatsApp and Tamil workflows |
| Meta (WhatsApp Business AI) | Built-in automation, improving fast | A rising tide that commoditises generic bots, so go vertical |
| Freelancers and agencies | One-off bots | You win by specialising in one vertical and owning outcomes |
