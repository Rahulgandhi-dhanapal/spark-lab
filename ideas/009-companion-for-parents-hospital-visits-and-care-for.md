# Companion for parents: hospital visits and care for parents whose children live elsewhere

**The spark** (one or two lines)
A trusted companion service for parents whose children live in another city or abroad: hospital visit accompaniment, medicine management, help with phones and banking, weekly check-ins with a report to the children.

**Why it's interesting**
1. India's over-60 population is heading toward about 20% by 2050.
2. Many Chennai parents have children in Bangalore, the US or the Gulf, who worry and can't be there.
3. The children pay, the parents benefit: a clear buyer with money and real pain.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Offer 5 free hospital-visit accompaniments to friends' parents and write down every problem along the way.

### ❌ What's wrong
1. **Too many services at once:** hospital visits, medicines, tech help, banking and check-ins. Each needs different people and has different risks.
2. **The buyer and the user are different people.** Children pay, but proud parents often refuse "a stranger to look after me."
3. **Trust and liability:** you send people into homes and handle health information and sometimes money. One bad incident ends the business.
4. **Hiring is the product.** Your quality equals the quality of your companions, and that's an operations problem, not a coding one.
5. **Funded players exist:** elder-care companies like Emoha, Samarth and Anvayaa offer subscriptions to families.

### 🤔 Why it's wrong
- Subscriptions ask parents to accept ongoing help, which is emotionally hard. A **single task** ("someone to come with me to Apollo on Thursday") is easy to accept.
- Two young founders don't yet have the credibility parents and children need, so trust has to come from structure: verification, reports and references.

### 🔧 How to make it right
1. **Start with one wedge: hospital visit accompaniment,** priced per visit. The companion books the slot, takes the parent there, handles the queue and pharmacy, and sends the children a summary with prescription photos.
2. **Recruit carefully:** nursing students, retired teachers or ex-hospital staff, with police verification and training. Always the same companion for the same parent.
3. **The report to the children is your product:** after each visit, send what the doctor said, medicines, the next appointment and a photo. Software helps here.
4. **Partner with one hospital** for referrals ("Need someone to accompany your parent?").
5. **Grow from there:** visits lead to medicine management, then weekly check-ins, then a subscription, but only once trust is earned.

### 🌍 The space
| Player | What they do | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Elder-care subscription companies (e.g. Emoha, Samarth, Anvayaa) | Packaged care for parents of children living away | The market is validated. Don't fight subscriptions head-on; win one task |
| Home nursing agencies | Medical care at home | Different, more regulated, more expensive |
| Relatives, neighbours, drivers | The informal default | Your real competitor. Beat it on reliability and reporting |

**Next step:** 5 free accompanied visits. If 3 families ask "can you do this again?", start charging.
