# Review replies in Tamil / Tanglish for Chennai shops

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Reply to every new Google review for Chennai shops in the owner's own voice and language (Tamil, English or Tanglish). I draft with an LLM, the owner approves on WhatsApp and posts. Expands #17.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: owners know replies bring customers but have no time.
2. Buyer: restaurants, salons and clinics with 50+ reviews; Rs 499 a month.
3. Edge: replies in the owner's voice and language; big tools do Tamil badly.
4. Fit: done by hand with an LLM and WhatsApp; build the tool only after people pay.
5. Same restaurants as Trailbite.

**First step, if I were to try it**
4-week test: 10 shops, 2 weeks free, then ask for month two. Yes = 3 of 10 pay. Plan: https://claude.ai/artifact/U1BDKGMKJWkxoCvXk6kcE4
