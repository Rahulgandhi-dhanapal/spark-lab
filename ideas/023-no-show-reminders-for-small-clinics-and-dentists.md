# No-show reminders for small clinics and dentists

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Tamil and English appointment reminders with "reply 1 to confirm" for one-doctor clinics, dentists and physios.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: a missed appointment is lost money and an empty chair.
2. Buyer: small clinics; Rs 999 a month.
3. Edge: local language, WhatsApp, the receptionist sees who confirmed.
4. Fit: tomorrow's patient list (name and time only, no medical data) plus WhatsApp messages.

**First step, if I were to try it**
3 clinics for 4 weeks. Yes = no-shows drop by a third and 2 pay.
