# Fee and attendance reminders for tuition centres

**The spark** (one or two lines)
WhatsApp-only fee and attendance reminders, in Tamil, for small tuition centres (30 to 200 students).

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: tutors chase parents for fees every month and feel awkward doing it.
2. Buyer: tuition centre owners; Rs 300 to 1,000 a month.
3. Edge: no heavy app to learn; Tamil, WhatsApp only. Big school apps are too much for them.
4. Fit: a sheet of students and fees plus polite automatic reminders.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Do it by hand for 5 centres for one month. Yes = fees come in faster and 3 pay.
