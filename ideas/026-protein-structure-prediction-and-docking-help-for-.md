# Protein structure prediction and docking help for labs

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Protein structure prediction and docking help: a lab sends a sequence or mutation and gets the predicted 3D structure, mutation effects and ligand docking, explained with clear pictures.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Prediction tools and the AlphaFold database are free, but many labs can't run or interpret them.
2. Buyer: academic labs, enzyme and protein researchers.
3. Edge: interpretation and clear reporting, not the run itself.
4. Watch-outs: check tool licences before charging (some weights are non-commercial); a predicted structure is a hypothesis.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Redo structure + docking for 3 proteins from published papers, make a sample report, offer 3 labs one job each. After 3 sequencing projects.
