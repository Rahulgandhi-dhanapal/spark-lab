# Plant-compound virtual screening for drug-lead research

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Careful virtual screening of plant / natural compounds against disease targets for drug-lead research: docking, binding checks and ADMET (drug-likeness) predictions, reported honestly.

**Why it's interesting**
1. This kind of study is very common in Indian theses and papers, and much of it is weak.
2. Buyer: researchers working on herbal and natural compounds.
3. Edge: rigour and honest reporting.
4. Watch-outs: screening only suggests candidates; needs a medicinal chemist partner; no health claims.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Redo one published screening study properly and compare, then offer it to 3 researchers.
