# CoolRoof Chennai: heat-proofing for homes and small shops

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Reflective cool-roof coating, shading and ventilation fixes for homes, tin-roof shops and small workshops, so they stay cooler in Chennai's heat.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Heat waves are getting more frequent and longer.
2. Cool roofs can bring indoor temperatures down by a few degrees for little money (Ahmedabad's heat programme and Telangana's cool roof policy use them).
3. Cheap for customers, nothing digital to build, and it can be measured with simple sensors.

**First step, if I were to try it**
Coat one tin-roof shop for free, measure inside temperature before and after for 2 weeks, and use the numbers to sell.

### ❌ What's wrong
1. **Paint companies and painters can already do this.** Major brands sell heat-reflective roof coatings, and any painter can apply them.
2. **Seasonal demand:** people buy in April and May when it's unbearable, and forget by July.
3. **The poorest people need it most and can pay least.** Tin-roof homes and shops benefit the most but have the least money.
4. **The benefit is hard to prove.** "It feels cooler" doesn't close sales.

### 🤔 Why it's wrong
- The idea competes on **installation**, where you have no advantage, instead of **proof and finance**, where you do.
- Selling one roof at a time to individuals has a high sales cost and low ticket size.

### 🔧 How to make it right
1. **Measure everything:** a ₹500 temperature sensor before and after, plus a simple report ("your shop is 4°C cooler at 2 pm"). That's your differentiator and your marketing.
2. **Target bigger roofs (B2B):** small factories, warehouses, schools and workshops. One sale covers 5,000 sq ft, and for factories, cooler workers mean better productivity.
3. **Find funders for low-income roofs:** corporate CSR budgets, NGOs and municipal heat-action programmes pay for impact *with data*, which you'd have.
4. **Sell before the heat:** pitch in January and February, install in March.
5. **Partner with one paint brand** for wholesale prices and credibility.

### 🌍 The space
| Player | What they do | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Mahila Housing Trust (Ahmedabad) | Cool roofs for low-income homes at scale | Proof that it works, funded by grants and CSR |
| Telangana Cool Roof Policy | State-level cool-roof push | Policy demand is coming. Watch Tamil Nadu |
| Paint brands' cool-roof products | Sell the coating | Suppliers, not competitors |
| Local painters | Cheap application | You win on data and funding, not labour |

**Next step:** one free roof plus two weeks of temperature data. If the drop is 3°C or more, you have a pitch deck in one chart.
