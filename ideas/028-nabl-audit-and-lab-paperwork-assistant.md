# NABL audit and lab paperwork assistant

**The spark** (one or two lines)
AI assistant for testing labs preparing for NABL (ISO/IEC 17025) accreditation: drafts SOPs, checks calibration and training logs for gaps, audit-readiness report.

**Why it's interesting**
1. Pain: audits are stressful and paperwork takes hours every week.
2. Buyer: food, water, pathology and pharma QC labs.
3. Edge: understanding lab workflows plus AI document checks.
4. Watch-outs: must learn NABL requirements well; slow sales.

**First step, if I were to try it**
One paid audit-readiness check done by hand. Pass = they pay and refer another lab.
