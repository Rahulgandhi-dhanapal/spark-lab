# FixAgain: doorstep repair and refurbishment

**The spark** (one or two lines)
Doorstep repair for mixers, fans, inverters and small appliances, plus refurbishing and reselling repaired items.

**Why it's interesting**
1. E-waste rules and rising prices make repairing a better deal than replacing.
2. Good repair people are getting rarer and harder to find.
3. Refurbished goods are a growing market.

**First step, if I were to try it**
List the 5 most common appliance breakdowns in our families and see which ones we could fix ourselves.

### ❌ What's wrong
1. **Urban Company already does doorstep appliance repair,** along with brand service centres and the neighbourhood repair shop.
2. **Low ticket size:** a mixer repair is ₹200–600. After travel time, margins disappear.
3. **Technicians are the bottleneck,** which is the same problem every repair company has.
4. **Refurbishing has inventory risk:** you buy broken items, fix them, then hope they sell.

### 🤔 Why it's wrong
- "Repair everything" means competing everywhere with no edge. Repair businesses win by **specialising** in one high-value category where expertise is scarce.

### 🔧 How to make it right
Narrow to one category where skills are scarce and tickets are bigger:
1. **Inverters and home UPS batteries.** This overlaps with BatteryDoc (same skills, safer chemistry), and every Chennai home has one.
2. **Or refurbished laptops for students.** Buy old office laptops in bulk, upgrade the SSD and RAM, and sell with a 6-month warranty. Higher ticket size, and you understand the tech.
3. **Or fold it into BatteryDoc** as a "battery and power" repair brand instead of keeping it separate.

### 🌍 The space
| Player | What they do | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Company | Doorstep repair marketplace | Owns the general case. Don't fight it |
| Cashify and refurb marketplaces | Refurbished phones and laptops | Refurbishing works at scale with standard grading and warranty |
| Brand service centres | Warranty repair | Slow and expensive after warranty, which is the gap |
| Local repair shops | Cheap, trusted locally | Possible partners, or recruits |

**Next step:** merge this with BatteryDoc as "power repair" (inverter batteries, UPS, e-scooter packs), or park it.
